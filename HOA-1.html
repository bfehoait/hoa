About the Bellefort HOA Portal

A proposed community website where verified homeowners can find approved updates, follow service requests and discover opportunities within Bellefort. The initial phase includes announcements and events, approved project updates and records, request tracking, and the Livelihood program. Meeting and task tools will support authorised officers and committees.

Project status: Working demo and readiness review. Pilot scope, funding, privacy controls and formal handover remain subject to the appropriate approvals. Demo access does not mean the production system has been approved.

Livelihood in one community link

The Livelihood Committee will manage eligibility checks, listing review, corrections and updates. The proposed five selections are Business Directory, Marketplace Buy and Sell, Garage Sales, Booth Events, and Job Openings and Referrals. Registration is free and voluntary for eligible, verified homeowners.

Participants can promote their offers and choose business-page, website, Messenger, Viber or WhatsApp links. Job applications and CVs should go privately to the listed employer or referrer. External chats stay in the selected app; the portal will not automatically read or import them.

Your information and your choices

Portal participation is voluntary. To create an account, willing homeowners will need to provide the minimum details needed for verification and the services they choose. This may include a name, contact details and home address; business details are needed only when creating a listing.

Before you submit information, the relevant privacy notice will explain the purpose, responsible entity, lawful basis, recipients, retention, privacy contact and your rights. Optional publication of contact or business information will have separate, specific choices. Home addresses, identity documents and private household records will not appear in member listings by default.

Existing QR-code or form submissions are not blanket permission to reuse data in this portal. Each import must have a compatible purpose and a valid lawful basis. Existing or assisted HOA service channels should remain available for homeowners who do not join.

Security with evidence

The proposed safeguards include individual logins, restricted household and role access, two-step authentication for administrators, controlled publication, activity records, protected backups and tested recovery. We will report which controls have been verified before opening the relevant services.

Implementation must follow the Data Privacy Act, RA 10173, and applicable NPC requirements. A privacy notice, a consent checkbox or a cloud subscription does not by itself prove full compliance. NPC registration, where required, will be assessed and documented; no certification or registration is claimed without evidence.

New homeowner? Register and the admin office will verify you before your account is opened. Your data is stored securely online.
